08.21 · First video creative · Meta · TikTok

A day with a mother in her forties — health checkup POV vlog

The first video creative I made after joining. It follows a mother in her forties as aches pile up, showing three habits — supplements, squats while brushing, drinking water — and closes on the app keeping the record for her. Three videos went live the same day alongside a mentor-quote and a squat-question version, and only this vlog got traction. I rebuilt it in AHEALTH branding across three sizes on Aug 31 and ran it again; that version delivered half of all installs across the three.
