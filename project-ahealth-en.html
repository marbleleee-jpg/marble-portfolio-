Project 03 · Content Marketer · Gravity Labs · Sep 2026

21 promotion creatives,
made in two days

A relaunch promotion for AHEALTH, the healthcare rewards app formerly known as MoneyWalk. New sign-ups were guaranteed a gift, anything from a Bacchus energy drink to a Galaxy Watch, over three days. The growth team owned the promotion plan and marketing supported it by producing the ad creatives. I made 6 image concepts in two sizes and 9 videos across three formats — object-only 3D, AI person, real person — in two days, and ran them on Meta and Moloco. It ranked first in both volume and efficiency among everything live that period.

Image Creatives — 5 of 6 hooks

Same offer, different first line, different creative. I split them by what leads: the product (Bacchus, Galaxy Watch), the absence of conditions (Just take it), the odds (No blanks), and anticipation (Mystery box). Each concept was built at 1080×1080 and 1200×628 to cover both placements.

Among the videos, testimonial hooks that put the real product up front — the Galaxy Watch, the Bacchus bottle — performed strongest. Person-led creatives beat object-only ones on installs, and among those the AI-generated person's testimonial came first. On the banner side, one square unit that stated the 100% win outright took 134 installs with 55% converting to sign-up, the highest conversion of any creative.

What I changed in the next creatives
01
Matching expectations beats a stronger hook

An earlier banner test lifted CTR 3.5× and installs fell to 40%. Once I saw that a click-winning line drops people when it clashes with the store page, this promotion put the app's real reward straight into the hook.

02
A/B live-action against AI on the same hook

The FOMO hook did 3.6× more installs as live-action, while the testimonial won as AI. Within one campaign I saw side by side that the answer depends on the hook. It needs more cases before it hardens into a rule.

03
Each format is good at a different stage

Videos with a person speaking drove the most installs; banners took the people who installed through to sign-up. One square banner stating the 100% win outright converted 55%, first among all creatives. I read results on installs and IPM, and keep CTR for diagnosing why something failed.
